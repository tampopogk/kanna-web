Kanna 4 · for macOS

Build software you understand, at a pace you can sustain, while having fun.

Kanna is a terminal for macOS that runs your coding agents as tasks, each in its own worktree, each showing you what it did when it's done. Its terminals outlive the app.

Apple Silicon Intel

Requires macOS 15 or later

I.

Understand

Know what your agents built.

Agents write code faster than you can read it. Kanna keeps you on top of it: every task shows its work, explains it on the lines, and asks you what landed.

i.

Tasks present their work.

When a task is done, its agent lays out its Desk to show you what changed and why: the spec, the mockup, the diff, comments on the key lines. Pull it hours later and it's waiting as it was left.

ii.

Explained on the lines.

Ask how something works and the agent opens the file in a code tab and comments on the lines it's talking about, the rest dimmed. For the bigger picture, a markdown doc with mermaid diagrams.

iii.

Review like a colleague.

Comment on a spec or a mockup and the agent beside it reads, answers and fixes.

iv.

A quiz on what landed.

Five questions on what merged into main today, about two minutes. Miss one and the code opens at the line, with the reason on it. No streaks, no score to protect.

II.

Sustain

Work at a pace you can keep.

Say what you want once. Kanna runs the rest a few at a time, and keeps your attention for one task.

i.

Plan with one agent.

Every window has a planning agent. Tell it what you want; it reads the code, asks until the decisions are settled, and writes the tasks.

ii.

The backlog runs itself.

Backlog tasks start on their own, three at a time, oldest first, and stop when they're done or need you. One that's finished when you pull it is a happy surprise.

iii.

One Desk, a short Shelf.

One task on the Desk: its agent on the left, its work in tabs on the right. Two more wait on the Shelf, still running. Three at a time, by design.

iv.

Tasks that wait their turn.

A task can wait for others, and won't start until they're done.

v.

Terminals that never die.

Quit Kanna, crash it, upgrade it: every shell and agent keeps running, its screen intact.

III.

Fun

And enjoy it.

A fast, good-looking terminal with everything you're building beside it.

i.

Everything side by side.

Terminals drawn by Ghostty, web pages, markdown with mermaid diagrams, highlighted code and the iOS Simulator, in windows, tabs and splits.

ii.

Agents drive it too.

The kanna CLI opens, arranges and shows it all, for you and for your agents.

iii.

Bring your agent.

Claude Code, Codex, Copilot, OpenCode and more.

$ kanna open --name server --cmd "npm run dev" --split right --size 1/3
$ kanna open web http://localhost:3000 --tab
$ kanna open md docs/spec.md --tab
$ kanna open ios --app MyApp
