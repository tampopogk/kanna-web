Kanna 4 for macOS

Build software you understand, at a pace you can sustain, while having fun.

Kanna is a software development platform for working with coding agents. It keeps you connected to your code, engaged in the work, and sane.

Apple Silicon Intel

Requires macOS 15 or later

01
plan

Better tasks, up front.

Most tools hand you a prompt box. In Kanna, the planning agent helps you write better task prompts.

Each task starts with enough detail to go from idea to production code, often in one go. You're still the bottleneck, but for design decisions, not for reviewing PRs.

02
model

See it before it's built.

Agents are great at toy examples. Hardened code takes longer. So each task starts with a model of the feature: a mockup or a prototype, with a markdown spec beside it. We call it reference-driven development.

02.1Iterate on the model.

A mockup changes in minutes, so you can try ideas fast without building them for real.

02.2Mark it up together.

Comment on the spec, the mockup or the prototype, and the agent refines it with you.

02.3Approve, then build.

Once you approve the model, the agent writes the production code to match it.

03
desk

Three tasks on your Desk. The rest in the background.

Watching ten agents at once is like drinking ten shots of espresso. Jumping from task to task feels fast, but you're spinning your wheels. Real work takes real concentration, and getting into flow takes time. So Kanna keeps you on one task and keeps the switching to a minimum.

03.1A Desk of three.

The task you're on, and two more on the Shelf. That's the limit, on purpose.

03.2The backlog works while you do.

Agents work through your backlog in the background, and stop when they're done or need you.

03.3Results, presented.

Call a task up to your Desk and its agent has laid out what it did: the spec, the model, the diff, its notes. Agents choose which windows, tabs and docs to show you.

04
connected

Stay connected to your codebase.

Even when agents write most of it, it's still your code.

04.1Explained on the lines.

Agents explain their changes with notes on the lines, shown in Kanna, never added to your code.

04.2A daily quiz.

Take a break with a daily quiz on how your codebase actually works. Your results are kept, so later quizzes focus on your weak spots.
